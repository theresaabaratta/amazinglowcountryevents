What we bring to your site

Tents & Structures

Pole and frame tents sized for a garden ceremony or a 300-guest reception, staked and leveled by our crew to suit lawns, docks, and courtyards.

  • 20×20 to 40×100 frame tents
  • Sailcloth & pole tents
  • Clear-top & sidewall options
  • Flooring & sub-flooring

Tables & Seating

Farmhouse, round, and cocktail tables with chairs to match the tone of the day, from Chiavari gold to relaxed cross-back wood.

  • 60" & 72" round tables
  • 8ft farmhouse tables
  • Chiavari & cross-back chairs
  • Bar stools & lounge seating

Linens & Tabletop

Pressed linens, flatware, glassware, and china in Lowcountry-friendly neutrals, plus seasonal color for a rehearsal dinner or shower.

  • Linen & runner selection
  • China, flatware & glassware
  • Chargers & napkins
  • Full place-setting packages

Lighting & Power

String lights, uplighting, and dance-floor lighting, backed by generators and cabling for sites without reliable outdoor power.

  • Market & string lighting
  • Uplighting & string bulb kits
  • Portable generators
  • Cable ramps & distro

Booking with us, start to finish

Most orders are placed six to nine months ahead for peak wedding season, though we regularly turn around smaller gatherings in under two weeks.

01

Tell us about the site

Share your date, guest count, and venue — or send photos of your own backyard or dock so we can plan around it.

02

Build the order

We put together tenting, seating, and tabletop options within your budget, and adjust until the list feels right.

03

We deliver & set up

Our crew arrives ahead of your event to stake, level, and stage everything, walking the layout with you before we leave.

04

We handle breakdown

After your event, we return to strike the tent and collect everything — no folding tables at midnight.
